Rules

One stake — one ball

Choose a stake per ball, 1–3 balls and 5, 7 or 9 lines. Press SPIN. Holding it repeats bets.

Stopping on a multiplier

Stake per ball × its stopping cell multiplier. All ball payouts add up. Less than the stake is a partial return, not profit.

3X BALLS · green pocket

Three balls replace one. Their payouts add up. No extra stake.

10X BOOST · purple pocket

Main and bonus field multipliers become ×10 for this ball and the balls it creates. Another purple hit extends the effect; it does not multiply it again.

EX MULTI · yellow pocket

Selected main-field cells get ×10. The ball launches again: it must stop in an enhanced cell for the higher payout.

LUCKY SHOT · red pocket

The ball sinks, then launches into one of two Bonus fields. Every cell pays. 10X BOOST also applies here.

5, 7 or 9 lines

Lines change the field and multipliers, not the stake per ball. Change them between games.

Autoplay

Choose a shot count and optional stop conditions. Bonuses cost no extra stake. Hiding the game pauses it; returning resumes it. After resizing, tap the screen.

Good to know

Multipliers shown without bonuses.

Payouts and colours

10x or more plays music and flashing, including purple wins. Boosted green cells turn yellow from 1x and red from 10x. Purple means 10X BOOST.

Theoretical RTP

RTP is 97.45% on all lines, including bonuses. This is an average over many bets, not a guarantee for one game.

Maximum win

Maximum per paid ball: 5 lines — 1,200x with 10X BOOST, then LUCKY SHOT and 120x on the right Bonus field. On 7 / 9 lines: 6,000x / 8,500x with EX MULTI and 10X BOOST, in either order, then a hit on the boosted centre. Each ball can enter at most two pockets in total. Created balls inherit the entries already used.

Bonus queues

Bonuses play in turn; new bets wait for them. Purple and yellow boosts can combine. Yellow only boosts the main field.

Test build

The balance and player tables are for demonstration. Outcomes are random and do not depend on balance or previous wins. Speed does not change payouts.
