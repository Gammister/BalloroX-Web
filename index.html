Game rules

One stake — one ball

Choose your stake and 5, 7 or 9 lines. Tap Spin to launch one ball. Hold it for new launches every second; every 0.5 seconds in Quick Play.

Stopping on a multiplier

The payout is the original stake × the stopping cell multiplier. A return below your stake is a partial return, not a net win. Red and purple results play celebration music and flashing walls.

X3 BALLS · green pocket

A ball is pulled into the green pocket on the left. The cactus rises and flies to its counter. One symbol is enough: after preparation, the pocket releases three white balls. Their payouts are added together.

X10 BOOST · purple pocket

The top pocket collects one diamond. After it reaches the counter and other balls stop, the field and both rooms turn purple. Then the ball launches; its wins and those of its bonus descendants are multiplied by 10. The effect lasts until the whole chain finishes.

EX MULTI · yellow pocket

The right pocket collects one star. After it reaches the counter and other balls stop, moving highlights select cells and temporarily multiply their values by 10. The pocket releases the ball; a boosted payout requires stopping on an enhanced cell.

LUCKY SHOT · red pocket

The bottom pocket collects one flame. The ball blinks in the pocket while preparing, then sinks and appears between the two secret rooms without blinking. The rooms flash, one is selected and the ball launches into it. Every cell in both rooms pays; X10 BOOST also applies there.

5, 7 or 9 lines

Lines change the cells and their multipliers, not the stake per ball. See the current table below. Configuration can only change after active play finishes.

Autoplay

Choose the number of paid shots and start autoplay. Balls launch every 1 s, or every 0.5 s in Quick Play. Bonus preparation and play pause new bets. Bonus launches do not charge a new stake.

Good to know

Field values show the payout as a multiple of the original stake. Colour identifies the multiplier tier; it does not change the payout.

Payouts and colours

Green: up to 1x. Yellow: above 1x but below 10x. Red: 10x or more. Purple: active X10 BOOST. Only red and purple results trigger a big-win celebration. All bonus descendants belong to their original paid stake.

Theoretical RTP

This MVP has a theoretical RTP of 97.45% at 5, 7 and 9 lines, including bonus chains. It is an average over many stakes, not a promise for a session. Probabilities do not adapt to your balance or previous wins.

Field and secret rooms

The table shows normal values without boosts. X10 BOOST multiplies values by 10; EX MULTI temporarily boosts selected cells by another 10. A single-cell multiplier is not a cap on the total payout of a bonus chain.

Bonus queues

New bets lock when bonus collection begins. Already launched balls finish moving. Bonuses resolve in order: green → yellow → red → purple. The number above a pocket counts waiting balls, excluding the current ball. A continuous queue sends its symbol to the counter only once.

Test build

This is a local MVP with a demo balance, not real-money play. Outcomes use fixed probabilities, followed by an existing physically computed trajectory. Animation and speed do not change payouts. Other-player tables are labelled as demo.
